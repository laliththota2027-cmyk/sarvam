How I built this page

I am an HR professional, so I built this the AI-native way: I designed it, an AI wrote the code under my direction, and I reviewed every round.

My thinking

  • Concept. Sarvam builds AI as a full stack, so I mapped my profile to a four-layer People Stack and one idea: GCC rigour, startup speed.
  • Research first. I read Sarvam's public launches and job posts, then turned each business front into a people problem and a move.
  • Honesty rules. Every figure comes from my CV. Gaps are stated, and the chat is told to say so when it does not know.
  • Iteration. I rejected the first version as generic and went through more than ten rounds of review on motion, colour, voice and wording.
  • Real voice. I explored AI voice cloning with ElevenLabs, then shipped my own recording instead.

Under the hood

  • AI pair-building. Built with Claude (Anthropic): I wrote the briefs, checked the renders and directed each revision.
  • One file. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework. GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis drive the scroll motion.
  • Hero. 6,210 particles drawn on a Canvas, one per transaction, morphing between a grid and a waveform.
  • Speech to text. The browser's SpeechRecognition API, in five Indian languages; Claude's dictation when opened on Claude.
  • Text to speech. The speechSynthesis API with device voices; Claude's voice when opened on Claude.
  • My intro. My recording is embedded in the file and played through the Web Audio API; an AnalyserNode makes the dots move with my voice.
  • Ask my profile. On Claude, a language-model call answers only from a fact sheet built from my CV. Elsewhere, questions are keyword-matched to prepared answers.
